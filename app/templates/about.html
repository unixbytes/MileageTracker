About Mileage Tracker

A simple, fast, and privacy-friendly way to log and review your driving mileage.

How to Use

  1. Open the Track page to start live tracking, or open Trips to manually log a trip.
  2. Select the trip type: Personal, Business, Medical, or Charity.
  3. Enter or confirm the vehicle name. You can manage vehicles in Settings.
  4. Start tracking to record GPS points automatically. Press Stop when finished.
  5. Open the trip detail page to view the recorded route.
  6. Use Reports to filter trips by month and trip type, and export data to CSV from there.

Features

  • Live Tracking: Records GPS points and calculates distance automatically. Distance is shown in your chosen unit.
  • Trip Types: Personal, Business, Medical, and Charity.
  • Settings: Change the measurement unit between Miles and Kilometers. Add, remove, and set a default vehicle. Update your profile/email and change your password.
  • Reports: Filter trips by month and trip type. Export trips as CSV.
  • Trip Photos: Attach a photo to a trip record.
  • Dark Mode: Toggle dark mode from the top navigation.

Privacy

Your data remains on your local system. No information is sent to external servers or third-party services. This application does not collect, store, or share personal data outside of your own environment.
